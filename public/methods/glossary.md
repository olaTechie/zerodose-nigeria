# Glossary

## ABM: Agent-Based Model
A simulation that represents individual decision-makers and runs forward in time under specified rules. Here, 38,490 virtual children in 1,283 survey clusters, linked to the risk model and the economic model.

## Access-Constrained
A community type identified by k-means clustering: survey clusters with longer travel times and lower engagement with services. **39.1%** of clusters in NDHS 2024.

## AUC-ROC: Area Under the Receiver Operating Characteristic curve
A measure of classification performance from 0.5 (random) to 1.0 (perfect). The risk model's held-out AUC is 0.947 (95% CI 0.930 to 0.961).

## CNA: Coincidence Analysis
A configurational method that identifies which combinations of conditions are necessary and sufficient for an outcome. Used here to identify which simulated intervention packages reach 80% DTP1 coverage by month 60.

## Community engagement
Caregiver-targeted communication and mobilisation led by local leaders or community health workers.

## Consistency
In CNA, how reliably a condition (or combination) goes with the outcome. Necessity consistency of 1.0 means every case with the outcome has the condition.

## Coverage (CNA sense)
In CNA, the proportion of cases with the outcome that the condition accounts for. Distinct from vaccination coverage.

## DALY: Disability-adjusted life year
A measure of health loss combining years of life lost and years lived with disability. Costs are reported per DALY averted.

## DHS: Demographic and Health Survey
Nationally representative household surveys conducted in low- and middle-income countries; Nigeria has waves in 2003, 2008, 2013, 2018 and 2024.

## DTP1: First dose of diphtheria-tetanus-pertussis vaccine
In Nigeria given as pentavalent vaccine. A child with no DTP-containing dose is zero-dose.

## Efficiency frontier
The packages that are not dominated (more costly and less effective than an alternative) or extendedly dominated. Incremental cost-effectiveness ratios are calculated along it.

## Engagement class
One of four caregiver classes from latent class analysis, distinguished mainly by contact with routine services: low engagement; intermediate, COVID-19 vaccinated or unwilling; high engagement; intermediate, willing but unvaccinated for COVID-19.

## IA2030: Immunization Agenda 2030
WHO's framework for global immunization 2021 to 2030.

## ICER: Incremental cost-effectiveness ratio
The extra cost per extra DALY averted when moving from one package to the next.

## LCA: Latent Class Analysis
A statistical method that groups individuals into unobserved classes based on several categorical indicators. Used here to describe caregivers' engagement with services (ten indicators specified, nine used, four classes).

## LISA: Local Indicators of Spatial Association
Per-location version of Moran's I that flags hotspots (High-High) and coldspots (Low-Low).

## Linked simulation
The agent-based model, parameterised from the risk model, the engagement classes and the community typology, and feeding Coincidence Analysis and the economic evaluation.

## Moran's I
A measure of spatial autocorrelation. Values near +1 indicate strong clustering of similar values; values near 0 indicate spatial randomness.

## NDHS: Nigeria Demographic and Health Survey
The Nigerian DHS; the primary data source for this study.

## Outreach
Mobile vaccination teams that travel to communities outside fixed facility catchment. The S1 intervention component.

## Posterior median
The 50th percentile of a parameter's posterior distribution from the simulation's calibration.

## Reference
A community type identified by k-means clustering: survey clusters with shorter travel times and higher engagement. **60.9%** of clusters in NDHS 2024.

## S0, S1 to S5
S0 status quo; S1 mobile outreach; S2 community engagement; S3 supply-chain reinforcement; S4 outreach + engagement; S5 the full package (outreach + engagement + supply-chain reinforcement).

## SHAP: SHapley Additive exPlanations
A method that allocates a machine-learning model's prediction across its input variables. It describes how the model uses each variable, not causal effects.

## Supply-chain reinforcement
Cold-chain repair, stock buffering and routine restocking to prevent stock-outs.

## Zero-dose
A child aged 12 to 23 months who has received no dose of a DTP-containing vaccine.
